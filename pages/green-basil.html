Intro

Green Basil Restaurant, a family owned Thai restaurant located in Burnaby, BC. Me and my team were tasked with redesigning their website to help cater to their fundamentally changing customer base post-pandemic.

The Problem

Garnering key insights from GB and their customer base, I determined the customer-bases we needed to shift our design focus toward. These were the key findings :

Nearby Office Workers

As workers transition back to a in-person work schedule, we needed to entice their once most prominent customer base back to GB.

Dine-In Patrons

To grow this audience, we needed to design the website to frame GB as a upscaled night-time dining destination.

Takeout and Delivery

GB’s most prominent customer base, they needed a concise directory to GB’s delivery and takeout services

Research

I conducted precedent research looking at how upscaled restaurants designed their websites to showcase their food, ambiance and ethos. These were my key findings:

Photography First

Striking food, drink and interior photos created memorability.

Concise Navigation

Key pages were always easy to navigate within 2 clicks.

Refined Colour Palettes

Primary + Tonal colours created an elegant look.

Solution

Presenting, the re-designed Green Basil website! Visit it today at greenbasilrestaurant.ca. We first created wireframes before shifting to high-fidelity mock-ups designing in Figma. Following usability testing of the prototypes, we revised our mock-ups as the website was developed.

Homepage

The homepage showcases a gallery of the sites’ key pages, designed to hook users in with its imagery and allow efficient navigational routes.

Menu

Having an extensive menu, these pages were designed with simplified navigation in mind, made possible with the nav-menu.

Order Online

The order online page allows efficient redirection to external delivery services as well as featuring key takeout information.

Contact

The contact page was designed to allow users to easily make inquiries to the restaurant.

Reflection

After implementation, the site was able to garner 10,000+ unique visits. This was my first UX/UI website project with a real-world client and I was able to garner many new skillsets such as cross-communication between me and the web developers, creating design systems and component libraries from scratch, and conducting usability testing.
